In Summary

AI governance is the study of how humanity should manage the transition to increasingly powerful artificial intelligence: what rules, institutions, and norms would make that transition safe and broadly beneficial, rather than dangerous or narrowly captured by a few actors.

Allan Dafoe laid out this research agenda in 2018 for the Centre for the Governance of AI at Oxford, and it remains one of the clearest maps of the field. He splits the work into three areas, shown below. Explore each one, or use the sidebar. Every subsection links directly to the matching page in the original paper.
