In Summary

Technical Landscape

What AI can actually do, how fast it might progress, and how hard it will be to make it safe.

Imagines what transformative AI capabilities could emerge, in what order, and what strategic character they might have.

1. Rapid and Broad Progress

Asks whether AI capability will jump suddenly across many tasks at once, or arrive slowly and unevenly, since the timing changes which governance strategies still work.
