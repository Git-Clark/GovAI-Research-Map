AI Politics

How governments, firms, and the public will compete and cooperate as AI capabilities spread.

Looks at how AI could shift the balance of power within a country, between governments, elites, and ordinary citizens.

Explores how AI could tip domestic power toward authoritarian control, or instead strengthen democratic accountability.
